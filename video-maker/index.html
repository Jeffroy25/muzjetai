About us

Thumbnail maker is a free tool for people who publish videos and need a clear, bold thumbnail without opening a design app. Choose a layout, add your own pictures, type a headline and download a 1280 × 720 PNG that is ready for YouTube.

It runs in your browser. There are no accounts to create, and your pictures are not uploaded to our servers.

What it does

  • Gives you six news-style layouts, from a split screen to a TV-style lower third.
  • Reads the layout and colors of thumbnails from a channel you admire and suggests a similar setup.
  • Builds a style profile (layout, colors, mood) from three thumbnails you pick.
  • Writes an original script of 5 to 60 minutes with AI, based on the topics and tone of three videos you pick.
  • Turns pictures into a slideshow video of 5 to 60 minutes with zoom motion, transitions, music and a voiceover, saved as an MP4.
  • Keeps your title, description and notes on your device while you work.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't copy anyone's thumbnail. The channel feature looks at small reference images for layout and color only. The pictures, headline and wording in your thumbnail are always yours.

It also doesn't upload to YouTube for you or promise any number of views.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026.

This site is built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your pictures

Pictures, music and voiceover files you choose are read and combined inside your browser, including when you make a video. They are not sent to our servers or stored by us.

What is saved on your device

If you use these features, your browser keeps the following in its local storage: your video title, description and notes, and the YouTube or Anthropic API keys if you choose to save them. The AI Video Studio and script writer also auto-save your script, scenes, chosen pictures and effects in your browser so you can continue later. Nothing else is stored. You can delete all of it with the button below or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Services we connect to

  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts. Google receives your IP address when your browser requests them.
  • YouTube and Google APIs are contacted only when you use the channel feature. They receive the link or channel you enter, your API key and your IP address. YouTube's image servers supply the reference thumbnails you see.
  • jsDelivr or esm.sh delivers the video library when you export a video. They receive your IP address.
  • Stock media sites (Pixabay, Pexels, Unsplash, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons) are searched through our server function when you use auto stock media. They receive the search words, not your script. Chosen pictures are saved in your browser, with their credits.
  • Anthropic receives your topic, notes, tone, the titles, descriptions and tags of the videos you picked, and your API key when you use the script writer. Your script is generated by their service.
  • Email or a form service receives your message if you use the contact form.

These services have their own privacy policies.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies itself and has no accounts. Third-party services listed above may set their own. If analytics or advertising are added later, this policy will be updated before they go live.
