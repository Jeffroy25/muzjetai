Text to Speech AI

Turn scripts into natural voiceovers directly in your browser. Built around the open Kokoro model, with smart chunking for long narration and an Adam male voice option.

FREE • LOCAL AI

1. Your narration

First use downloads the open model once and caches it in your browser.

2. Voice & engine

How it stays free
Speech is generated on the user's device with Kokoro. Your script is not sent to a paid TTS API. WebGPU is used when available, with WASM fallback.
Long scripts
The app breaks long text into sentence-sized chunks and combines the audio into one WAV file, so 10-minute narration and larger scripts are supported.
Model/library: Kokoro-82M • Apache-2.0. Browser performance depends on the user's device.
